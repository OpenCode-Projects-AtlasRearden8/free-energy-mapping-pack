The Five-Level Task Matcher

Here's a quiet idea: tasks don't all ask for the same you. Email from the hospital asks for a different version of you than calling your friend back does. This sheet is a matchmaker between the task and the energy it honestly takes.

Everything gets a level from 1 to 5. Then, when a day hands you a certain amount of energy, you stop begging yourself to do things the wrong version of you is holding.

  • 1Mind-dump your tasks onto this sheet. Gather them from everywhere — notes app, head, sticky notes.
  • 2Give each task a level, 1 to 5, using the key. Be generous downward; a task you keep postponing is probably not a level 1.
  • 3When today's energy for a slot shows up (check your Weekly Energy Map), pick from that level's column. That's the whole trick.

Nothing here needs to be finished today. The matcher just keeps everything somewhere it belongs, so the level-1 version of 2 p.m. you has somewhere true to go.

One task per line. Leave blanks empty — blank rows are permission, not homework.
