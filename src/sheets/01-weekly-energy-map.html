The Weekly Energy Map

Hi. This sheet is for drawing a picture of your week the way it actually feels — not the way a calendar app wishes it felt.

Most planners assume you arrive each morning with a full tank. Your energy doesn't work like that, and that's not a character flaw. Some mornings are bright. Some afternoons are fog. This map lets you sketch both in the same place, honestly.

  • 1Print this page plus the next one.
  • 2Once or twice a day, jot a word in the matching time slot: goal — what you intend to do, and real — what your energy actually did.
  • 3Circle nothing. Judge nothing. You're collecting a weather report, not grading yourself.

By Sunday you'll have a gentle, honest record of where your energy really lives in a week. That record is a map. The other six sheets in this pack are ways to travel by it.

Photocopy freely. No signups, no accounts, no branding — this is for your kitchen table, not a waiting room.
