The Sunday Gentle Review

This is the sheet that connects the week. It's short on purpose — fifteen minutes with a warm drink, done once and then left alone.

It's not a performance review of your week, and it is emphatically not the energetic-Thursday-planner's Sunday ritual with a nicer font. There are no goals sections, no momentum language, no "crush next week." Just six warm prompts and a soft next step.

  • 1Pick a Sunday time that already exists: with the late-morning coffee, or early evening, or whatever slot your week naturally offers.
  • 2Answer briefly. Three words beats a paragraph. This sheet is a mirror, not an essay assignment.
  • 3Close by carrying forward exactly one energy lesson — and only one. Next week is new; nothing is owed.

When this sheet is used with the rest of the pack, it works like this: the map (sheet 1) collects weather, the matcher (2) keeps tasks sorted, and this sheet is where the two are gently compared against reality for a minute — then left alone till next Sunday.

One review a week beats none. Two reviews a week helps until it doesn't; you'll know.
