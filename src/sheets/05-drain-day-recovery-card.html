The Drain-Day Recovery Card

A drain day is different from a low-battery day. On a low-battery day, the tank is low and the menu (sheet 3) helps. On a drain day, something specific emptied the tank — a hard conversation, a surprising bill, a bad shift, news that fell heavy. Drain days need recovery, not productivity, and recovery has its own small steps.

Print this and fold or clip it wherever you'll see it. The idea is that on the day itself you do as little thinking as possible: the card already knows what to do. You just follow the next kindest line on it.

  • 1Fill in your personal recovery items while things are okay — the card works best pre-loaded with things that genuinely help you.
  • 2On a drain day, start at the top: pause first, then the floor of the low-battery menu, then a card item.
  • 3Say the card's one sentence out loud if you can. It's short on purpose: "Today is a recovery day. Small counts."

Recovery days aren't lost days. They're the maintenance schedule for the machine that does everything else you do.

Not medical advice, just logistics for the aftermath of a hard one. If drain days stack up day after day, that's information worth giving a professional.
