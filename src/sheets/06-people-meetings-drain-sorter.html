The People & Meetings Drain Sorter

This is possibly the most useful sheet in the pack, and it's slightly harder to be honest about than the others — because it's about people. Everyone's energy leaks somewhere around certain humans and certain meetings, and the leaks are rarely bearable to name.

The sorter isn't about judging anyone. Some people energize, some are neutral, some drain — and this can be true of genuinely wonderful people. It can even be true of people you love. Naming the pattern is how you stop being surprised by the empty feeling at 4 p.m.

  • 1List the people you spend time with and the meetings that recur. Guess first; refine next week.
  • 2Sort each into Energize / Neutral / Drain. Add a one-word "why" — the why is where the usable information lives.
  • 3For each Drain, pick a tiny action: shorter, later, with someone else in the room, walking instead of seated, or graced with a reschedule. Drain items don't need solving, just adjusting the dosage.

A drained calendar is not usually about busyness. More often, it's about dosage. You're not cutting people off — you're adjusting how much, and when.

Written privately, kept private. If you work in healthcare or anywhere with a roster, some drains are mandatory staffing — sort them anyway, honestly; the sheet doesn't require you to fix what you name.
